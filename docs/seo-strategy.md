## 3. Architecture & Rendering Model
- **Framework:** React 19 + TypeScript + Vite.
- **Routing & Deep Linking:** SPA routing with seamless browser history synchronization (`pushState` / `popstate`). Direct navigation to any page (e.g. `/persian-cats-banjara-hills`) correctly renders without 404.
- **Meta Management:** Reactive head tags updating `<title>`, `<meta name="description">`, `<meta name="keywords">`, `<link rel="canonical">`, and OpenGraph/Twitter social cards upon every route change.
- **Static Assets:** Static `/robots.txt` and `/sitemap.xml` placed in `/public` to ensure raw crawler indexing by Googlebot, Bingbot, and other search engines.
